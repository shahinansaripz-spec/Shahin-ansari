Research

Research interests

My research focuses on the theoretical and applied aspects of abstract differential equations, for both deterministic and stochastic systems.

I study qualitative properties such as existence, controllability, and optimal control, working mainly through abstract formulations of partial differential equations. Alongside this, I have an ongoing interest in numerical analysis, discretization methods, and stability theory.

Stochastic calculus

Qualitative behaviour of nonlinear stochastic differential and integro-differential equations, including impulsive and switched variants, in Hilbert-space settings.

Approximation of solutions

Faedo–Galerkin and finite-dimensional approximation schemes for abstract differential equations with non-instantaneous impulses.

Controllability of switched systems

Controllability of nonlinear stochastic impulsive switched systems in infinite-dimensional spaces.

Optimal control for fractional systems

Optimal control theory for fractional and multi-term fractional stochastic integro-differential equations, including systems with impulses and infinite delay.

Fractional differential equations

Existence and approximation results for fractional-order systems, extending to damped switched systems under stochastic and impulsive effects.

Numerical analysis & discretization

Discretization methods and approximation theory supporting the analytical results above, along with broader interests in stability of dynamical systems.
