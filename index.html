Mathematics · Stochastic & Fractional Systems

Shahin Ansari

I study how differential equations behave under randomness, memory, and abrupt change — and what that means for controlling the systems they describe.

I hold a PhD in Mathematics from IIT Mandi and currently teach quantitative courses at the School of Business, UPES, Dehradun. My work sits at the intersection of stochastic calculus, fractional calculus, and optimal control, mostly framed through abstract differential equations in infinite-dimensional spaces.

What I work on

Stochastic abstract differential equations

Existence, approximation, and controllability of solutions to differential equations set in Hilbert and Banach spaces, under randomness and impulsive change.

Fractional calculus & optimal control

Optimal control formulations for fractional and multi-term fractional integro-differential systems, including those with delay and impulses.

Switched & impulsive systems

Controllability and stability of switched systems, with particular attention to non-instantaneous impulses.

Currently

Teaching Business Statistics, Business Analytics, and Business Mathematics at UPES, while continuing research in collaboration with Prof. Muslim Malik and colleagues at IIT Mandi. I also review for Scientific Reports, Chaos, Solitons & Fractals, and Applied Mathematics in Science and Engineering.
